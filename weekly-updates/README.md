# Weekly Updates — Strategy

Strategy doc for the weekly progress posts that promote **MasterMind: Story Builder** to DM/GM communities AND position Erik as a Product Designer building real products with AI.

This doc captures the **current vision** for what these posts accomplish and how they get used. The operational pieces (channels, cadence, PROMPT structure) are deliberately deferred — see "What's still TBD" below.

---

## Two streams

The posts have **two distinct audiences with two distinct jobs**. Splitting them is a deliberate choice over trying to serve both with one stream — DMs and design/AI professionals want noticeably different content.

| Stream | Primary audience | Primary job |
|---|---|---|
| **DM-facing** | Hobbyist DMs and GMs, prospective testers + eventual users | Build broader audience over time so MasterMind has a warm community when it's ready for wider sign-ups. Not "source 5 testers this week" — slower-burn community-building. |
| **Designer/AI-facing** | Product designers, AI builders, professional community | Position Erik as a designer building real products with AI. Career-shaped audience-building. |

The two streams will end up with different channels, voices, cadences, and content. The DM-facing stream cares about *the tool that solves their continuity problem*. The designer/AI stream cares about *the making-of story and lessons learned from building with AI as a non-engineer*.

## Voice intent

**"Idiot's guide to working with AI"** — approachable, honest, lessons-from-confusion (not lessons-from-mastery).

The current [`PROMPT.md`](./PROMPT.md) produces a too-technical / engineer-diary voice. See [`drafts/_archive/2026-04-27-week-1-draft.md`](./drafts/_archive/2026-04-27-week-1-draft.md) as the canonical example of wrong-voice output — well-written for what it is, but reads as one engineer talking to other engineers. Not the voice for either stream's primary audience.

The existing PROMPT.md is closer to the **designer/AI stream's** voice goal than the DM stream's, so the rewrite work may produce a revised designer-stream prompt and a from-scratch DM-stream prompt.
