# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** 2026-05-26

**Last Updated:** 2026-07-19

This Privacy Policy describes what information MasterMind collects, why we collect it, who else handles it, how long we keep it, and the choices and rights you have.

MasterMind is operated by **Just Living The Dream LLC**, a Washington limited liability company (the "**Operator**", "we", "us", or "our"). You can reach us at **contact.mastermind.lab@gmail.com**.

This policy applies to your use of MasterMind, a beta web application for building tabletop role-playing game campaigns. It does not apply to anything you do outside the application — we have no access to and no interest in your activity elsewhere.

## 1. Information We Collect

### 1.1 Account information you provide

- **Email address** — required for sign-up, sign-in, password recovery, and to contact you about your account.
- **Password** — stored as a one-way hash. We cannot see your password.
- **Optional profile information** — display name (if set) and a profile photo (if uploaded).

### 1.2 Content you create in the app

Everything you create in MasterMind is stored so we can show it back to you on your next visit. This includes:

- Workspaces (campaigns) and their names
- Cards: titles, summaries, story notes, hidden lore, DM notes, and any custom sections
- Card thumbnail images and inspiration images you upload
- Connections between cards
- Text annotations on the canvas
- Custom card types you create
- Card positions and canvas layout

### 1.3 Information collected automatically

When you use MasterMind, certain information is collected automatically by the application and our hosting infrastructure:

- **IP address** (collected by our hosting provider, Vercel)
- **Browser type, operating system, screen size, time zone**, and other standard browser information
- **Pages and features you visit within MasterMind**
- **Errors and basic performance data**
- **Cookies and similar local-storage data** used to keep you signed in and remember your active workspace (see "Cookies and Local Storage" below)

### 1.4 Session recordings (see Section 2)

If you set up a free MasterMind account, your MasterMind sessions will be recorded using a service called **PostHog**. The next section explains exactly how this works; please read it carefully.

### 1.5 What we do NOT collect

- We do not collect financial information, payment details, or credit card numbers (the current product has no payments).
- We do not collect government IDs, social security numbers, or health information.
- We do not collect information about you from outside the application — we have no relationship with advertisers, data brokers, or third-party trackers that would feed us such data.

## 2. Session Recording — Important Disclosure

This section deserves your full attention.

**Recording is a condition of using MasterMind for free.** When you use MasterMind without a paid-tier subscription, session recording is part of the value exchange: you get free access to a product in active development, and we get visibility into how it actually gets used so we can improve it. **If you don't want your sessions recorded to help us make the product better, then the free version of MasterMind is not appropriate for you**.

### 2.1 What gets recorded

Our analytics provider **PostHog** records your sessions inside MasterMind. This means:

- **The visible contents of the application are captured** as you use it, including the **actual text you type into cards** (titles, summaries, story notes, hidden lore, DM notes, custom sections), the images you add, and your mouse and keyboard activity within the app.
- **Card content is recorded unmasked, by design.** We do not blur or hide the text you write into cards. The content of what tabletop game masters write is itself the research signal we're trying to learn from — how DMs structure their campaigns, what kinds of details they capture, and where they get stuck.
- **Recording only happens when you are signed in to MasterMind.** We have no access to anything you do in other tabs, in other applications, or anywhere outside the MasterMind window.

### 2.2 How recordings are used (and how they are not used)

- **Recordings are used solely for product research and improvement** — to understand how MasterMind is used and what to improve.
- **Recordings are never used for**: public demonstrations, marketing materials, advertising, social media posts, sales pitches to investors, training of generalized or third-party AI models, or sale to any third party.
- **Recordings are currently reviewed only by the MasterMind product development team**, which is limited to the Operator's internal product work. They are not shared with friends, other users, or outside reviewers, except for the sub-processors listed in Section 5 who help operate the service. If review access expands to additional personnel or service providers, we will update this Policy first.

### 2.3 Retention

Recordings are retained for **30 days** by PostHog and are then automatically deleted. We do not maintain backups of recordings beyond PostHog's own infrastructure.

### 2.4 If you change your mind

If you've already created an account and later decide you don't want to be recorded:

- You can ask us to remove your account. 
- We will delete any existing recordings tied to your account on request.
- You can also delete your account entirely at any time (see Section 10).

**Looking ahead:** if MasterMind eventually offers a paid tier, recording may be optional for paid users. This Privacy Policy describes how things work today; we will update it before any such change takes effect.

## 3. Why We Collect This Information

- **To provide the service.** Account data, content, and canvas state are required to sign you in, save your work, and render your campaign each time you visit.
- **To improve the product.** Aggregate usage data and session recordings help us see where the app is confusing, where people get stuck, what is working well, and what to fix next. The MasterMind product development team reviews recordings as part of designing the next iteration of MasterMind.
- **To improve MasterMind through machine learning.** Over time we may use aggregated and de-identified usage signals — which features you use, where you click, where errors occur, what kinds of campaign structures you build — to train and refine machine learning models that help us understand how MasterMind is used and recommend product improvements. These models exist only to improve MasterMind itself.
  - **We do not** train public, general-purpose, or third-party AI models on your private campaign content.
  - **We do not** currently share user data with any third-party AI vendor for training purposes.
  - If either changes — for example, if we add an AI feature that needs to learn from card content — we will update this Privacy Policy before doing so, add any new vendor to the sub-processor list in Section 5, and (where required by law) seek your consent.
- **To communicate with you.** Account-related emails (e.g., password reset, security alerts), responses to your support emails, and notices about material changes to the service or these documents.
- **For security and abuse prevention.** Detecting and responding to bad-actor activity, security incidents, and bugs that could put your data at risk.

## 4. Lawful Basis (for users in the EU / UK)

By creating an account and using MasterMind, you are entering into a contract under which recording is part of the service we provide.

If MasterMind eventually offers an option for users to use the service without session recording (for example, in a future paid tier), we will rely on **consent** (Article 6(1)(a)) for any user who chooses to participate in recording at that point. The current Privacy Policy describes how MasterMind works today.

## 6. Cookies and Local Storage

MasterMind uses cookies and browser local storage for the following purposes. None of these are used for advertising:

- **Authentication** — Supabase stores a session token in browser local storage so you stay signed in between visits.
- **Workspace state** — we store which workspace you last opened in browser local storage so you return to the same view.
- **Undo history** — recent undo/redo state is stored in browser session storage for the current tab.
- **Analytics** — PostHog uses cookies to identify your sessions across visits.

These mechanisms are essential to running the application. You can clear cookies and local storage through your browser settings, but doing so will sign you out and reset your last-opened-workspace memory.

## 10. Account and Data Deletion

You can delete your account at any time directly in MasterMind: go to your **Profile** page (click your avatar in the top-left of the canvas, then "View profile") and use the **"Delete account"** button in the Danger Zone section. If you can't access the in-app option for any reason, email **contact.mastermind.lab@gmail.com** and we'll handle it.

When you delete your account:

- Your workspaces, cards, sections, uploaded images, connections, and text annotations are deleted from Supabase.
- Your PostHog session recordings (if any) are deleted as part of the same flow — typically processed by PostHog within minutes.
- Some information may persist briefly in our sub-processors' backup systems (up to approximately 30 days) and in support email threads if you contacted us by email.
- We retain a minimal record (your email address and the date of deletion) for legal compliance and to prevent accidental re-creation of a deleted account.

## 12. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your information, including:

- **Encryption in transit** — all traffic between your browser and our servers uses HTTPS.
- **Encryption at rest** — your content is encrypted at rest within Supabase.
- **Password hashing** — passwords are hashed by Supabase Auth using industry-standard algorithms.
- **Row-level security** — database policies prevent one user from reading another user's data.
- **Restricted access** — administrative access to the Operator's infrastructure is limited and requires multi-factor authentication.

**However, no method of transmission or storage over the internet is perfectly secure.** We cannot guarantee absolute security. If we discover a breach affecting your personal information, we will notify you in accordance with applicable law.
